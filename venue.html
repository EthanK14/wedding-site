Indigo Hall & Events

Parking Information

Parking after 5:00 PM on Mon-Fri and all day on Sat. and Sun. is available at no charge in the QS/1 parking deck on Ezell Street, the Redsail (QS/1) parking lot on Daniel Morgan Avenue and the Magnolia Street Garage. Surrounding street parking is also available at no charge on Sat. and Sun. Time restrictions are strictly enforced during business hours for street parking on Mon.-Fri. Paid parking is available in the Magnolia Street Garage during normal business hours on Mon.-Fri.
